Bimba

In progress
Photo coming soon

About

A retro pocket digicam built around a XIAO ESP32S3 Sense, with a small TFT viewfinder, RTC-stamped photos, and a WiFi file manager. I'm designing a custom PCB for it.

What it will do

  • Take JPEG photos to microSD, with retro filters
  • Live viewfinder and gallery on a 1.8" display
  • Bake the date and time into each filename with an RTC
  • Share photos to a phone over its own WiFi hotspot with a QR code

Where it's at

Camera and SD firmware confirmed working on real hardware. Schematic is done and the PCB is in layout.

Built with

  • XIAO ESP32S3 Sense
  • ST7735 TFT
  • DS3231 RTC
  • LiPo
  • KiCad

Lipi

In progress
Photo coming soon

About

A low-cost pocket e-reader built around a XIAO ESP32-S3, a 3.7" e-paper display, and a microSD card for books. I wrote the firmware and I'm designing a custom PCB for it.

How it works

  • Streams books from microSD one page at a time
  • Wakes on a button press, turns the page, then goes back to deep sleep to save battery
  • Two buttons, with short and long presses covering the whole menu
  • Roughly $30 to 40 in parts, against about $129 for a commercial e-reader

Where it's at

Firmware compiles and flashes to hardware. Schematic is done and the PCB routing is in progress.

Built with

  • XIAO ESP32-S3
  • 3.7" e-paper
  • microSD
  • LiPo
  • KiCad
