Side Projects

Bimba

A retro pocket digicam built on a XIAO ESP32S3 Sense, with a small TFT viewfinder, RTC-stamped photos, and a WiFi file manager. Includes a custom PCB.

In progress: camera and SD firmware working, PCB in layout.

Lipi

A low-cost pocket e-reader built on a XIAO ESP32-S3, a 3.7" e-paper display, and a microSD card for books. Includes a custom PCB.

In progress: firmware flashed to hardware, PCB routing underway.
